Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Speech Pact account and data directly from the app, in just a few taps.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Speech Pact and, on the homepage, tap the gear icon in the top right corner to open Settings.
  2. Tap the first section, Account and Subscriptions.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page. Delete Account is right above the Sign Out button.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, recordings, and related data.
