Privacy Policy

What information Speech Pact collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Speech Pact ("we", "us") collects when you use the Speech Pact mobile application (the "App"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the App and give you useful feedback.

  • Account information. Email address, password (hashed), and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and name from Google.
  • Speech recordings and transcripts. The audio you record, the transcript generated from it, and the AI-generated feedback (score, filler-word count, awkward-pause count, words per minute, transcript markers, coaching cards, structure analysis, scorecard).
  • Practice activity. Topics chosen, durations selected, recording timestamps, streaks, and progress data.
  • Subscription information. Subscription state, plan, and renewal status synced from Apple via RevenueCat. We never see your full payment-card details — Apple handles payment.
  • Notifications. A push-notification token (Apple Push Notification Service) and your notification preferences if you enable reminders.
  • Device & technical data. Limited information needed to operate and secure the service: IP address (used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention), device model, OS version, App version, and crash logs.
  • Community content. If you participate in challenges or peer feedback, the comments, ratings, and chat messages you send through Stream Chat.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or any information not listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate the App, sign you in, sync your data across devices, and remember your preferences.
  • Transcribe your speech and produce AI feedback (this is the core feature you signed up for).
  • Track streaks, progress, and personal best scores.
  • Process subscriptions and entitlements through Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Send the practice reminders and notifications you've enabled.
  • Prevent abuse — for example, rate-limiting analysis requests by IP and account.
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve the App. We do not use your audio recordings or transcripts to train third-party AI models.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

3. AI Speech Analysis

When you tap "Analyse Speech," your audio recording is sent to our Supabase Edge Function and from there to AI providers acting as our processors:

  • Deepgram — primary speech-to-text transcription.
  • OpenAI — fallback transcription (Whisper) and fallback written-feedback generation if our primary providers are unavailable.
  • Google (Gemini) — analysis of the transcript to produce coaching feedback.

Each provider receives only what they need — the audio bytes (Deepgram/OpenAI) or the transcript and speech metrics (Gemini/OpenAI). We do not attach your name or email to those requests. These providers process the data on our behalf under their respective enterprise agreements and do not use your content to train their general-purpose models.

AI feedback is generated automatically and may be inaccurate. It is for practice and self-improvement and is not professional speech, language, or medical advice.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Account data, recording metadata, and feedback are stored in our Supabase (Postgres) database. Audio files are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets and accessed only through short-lived signed URLs tied to your account.

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Row-level security policies in our database restrict each row to the account that owns it; you cannot read or modify another user's recordings or feedback.
  • Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are stored hashed; we never see them in plaintext.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

5. Service Providers

We share data only with vendors we rely on to run the App. Each acts as our processor:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and serverless functions.
  • Deepgram, OpenAI, Google Gemini — speech-to-text and AI analysis (see Section 3).
  • Apple App Store & RevenueCat — subscription billing and entitlement sync.
  • Stream Chat — chat features inside community challenges.
  • Resend — sends one-time-passcode (OTP) verification emails.
  • Apple Push Notification Service & Expo — delivers reminders and notifications you have enabled.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Sharing Inside the App

If you join a community challenge or send peer feedback, your display name and the content you post (ratings, written feedback, chat messages) are visible to other members of that challenge. Your recordings are only shared with another member if you explicitly submit them to a challenge that requires it. Your email and account settings are never shown to other users.

7. International Transfers

Speech Pact and our processors operate primarily in the United States. If you use the App from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. and other countries where our processors operate. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards for such transfers.

8. Data Retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, recordings, transcripts, feedback, and related data from our active systems. Limited backups and rate-limiting / abuse-prevention logs may persist for a short retention window before being purged. Some records may be kept longer where required by law or to resolve disputes.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction. You can view and edit your profile from the Account settings inside the App.
  • Deletion. Delete your account from Settings or by emailing speechpact@gmail.com. This removes your data as described in Section 8.
  • Notifications. Toggle reminders in Settings → Notifications, or disable them at the OS level.
  • Microphone permission. You can revoke microphone access in iOS Settings at any time. Without it, the App cannot record or analyze speech.
  • Data export. Email speechpact@gmail.com and we will provide a copy of your account data in a machine-readable format.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as those under GDPR or the CCPA), including the right to object to processing, request restriction, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

10. Children's Privacy

Speech Pact is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact speechpact@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not show ads. We collect only the limited diagnostic information described in Section 1 to keep the service running and reliable.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will notify you in the App or by email before they take effect. Your continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions, requests, or privacy concerns? Email us at speechpact@gmail.com. We are a small indie team and will respond as soon as we can.
