Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — the yearly plan ($69.99/yr) starts with a 3-day free trial with full access to everything: all 2,000+ topics, instant feedback and word-level analysis. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then. The monthly plan ($7.99/mo) starts right away, without a trial.

What happens after my free trial?

When the 3-day trial ends, your subscription begins at $69.99/year and you keep unlimited practice. If you'd rather not continue, just cancel before the trial ends in the App Store and you won't be charged. The monthly plan has no free trial.

How does the feedback work?

After you record a take, Speech Pact transcribes your speech and analyzes it for filler words, pacing, pauses, repeated words and clarity. You'll get a marked-up transcript and specific, encouraging coaching tips within a few seconds.

What's included in my subscription?

Your subscription unlocks unlimited daily practice, all 2,000+ topics, instant feedback, full word-level analysis, streaks and reminders, and priority support.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the App Store. To manage or cancel, open the App Store app → tap your profile → Subscriptions → Speech Pact. Canceling stops future renewals; you keep Premium until the end of the current billing period.

How do streaks work?

Complete at least one practice session in a day to keep your streak alive. Your streak and best streak are tracked on a calendar so you can see your consistency over time. Streak days follow your local time zone.

I changed my email or forgot my password.

If you need help with your account, email us at support@speechpact.com from the address on your account and we'll help you recover it.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and data directly in the app — see our step-by-step instructions. If you run into any trouble, email us at support@speechpact.com and we'll take care of it.
